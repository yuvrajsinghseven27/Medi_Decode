Hospital Jargon Translated

"Acute Febrile Illness secondary to LRTI" Doctor's Diagnosis

Plain English: You had a severe chest infection (Lower Respiratory Tract Infection) causing high fever, cough, and tiredness. It has been treated with antibiotics.

"HbA1c: 7.8% • Dyslipidemia" Lab Findings

Plain English: Your 3-month average blood sugar is slightly high (aim for under 7.0%), and your blood fat/cholesterol levels require maintenance with diet and statins.

"Ambulate as tolerated, incentive spirometry q2h" Recovery Orders

Plain English: Walk around the house gently as much as your energy allows. Use the breathing exercise ball-device every 2 hours while awake to keep your lungs clear.

Red-Flag Warning Signs

  • Chest tightness, radiating pain to left arm
  • Fever returning above 101°F with chills
